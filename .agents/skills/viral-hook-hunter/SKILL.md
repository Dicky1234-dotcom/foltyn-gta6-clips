# Viral Hook Hunter: Selection Rules & Playbook

This skill directs the agent on how to find high-performing viral moments from raw long-form footage (gaming streams, VODs, podcasts, interviews) rather than making generic cuts.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: The 0-to-2 Second Cold Open
* **Never start with a warm-up, throat clearing, or generic "welcome back":**
  * The first 2.0 seconds dictate whether the user swipes away.
  * Cut directly onto the most shocking, funny, loud, or controversial sentence.
  * **The Teaser Front-Load Trick:** If the funniest line or payoff happens at second 40, extract a 1.5–2.0 second clip of the punchline/reaction and splice it to the very front ($0.0\text{s}–2.0\text{s}$) before cutting to the setup.

---

## 2. The 4 Viral Pattern Interrupt Archetypes
When scanning transcripts or watching audio energy, score every segment against these 4 triggers:

1. **The Extreme Emotional Spike:**
   - Screaming, uncontrollable laughter, sudden gasps, rage quits, disbelief.
   - Example (Gaming/GTA): *"NO WAY THEY PUT THIS IN THE GAME?! LOOK AT THIS!"*
2. **The Curiosity / High-Stakes Dilemma:**
   - Unresolved questions that force the viewer to stay until the end.
   - Example: *"I found a secret area in GTA 6 that Rockstar tried to ban..."*
3. **The Rapid Conflict / Banter Spike:**
   - Fast back-and-forth arguments, betrayal by teammates or NPCs, unexpected catastrophes.
4. **The "Wait for It" Absurdity / Glitch:**
   - Bizarre physics, AI NPC doing something unhinged, accidental chain reactions.

---

## 3. Strict Runtime & Narrative Arc
* **Duration Range:** **25 to 55 seconds** (Sweet spot: **30–45s** for TikTok/Shorts algorithms).
* **The 3-Beat Arc:**
  1. **Hook ($0.0\text{s}–2.5\text{s}$):** Immediate pattern interrupt + visual punch-in.
  2. **Escalation ($2.5\text{s}–30.0\text{s}$):** Rapid pacing, tension building, humor stacking.
  3. **Climax / Payoff ($30.0\text{s}–45.0\text{s}$):** The explosion, crash, punchline, or realization.
* **The Seamless Loop Mandate:**
  - Whenever possible, end the clip on a grammatical bridge that flows directly back into the opening word (e.g. closing with *"and that's the reason why..."* into opening with *"Rockstar deleted this"*).

---

## 4. Gaming & Livestream Scoring Metrics (GTA 6 / Foltyn Specifics)
* **Audio Energy Peaks:** Look for sudden volume increases ($\ge +12\text{ dB}$) over baseline indicating hype/screams.
* **Keyword Triggers in Transcripts:**
  - Words like `"glitch"`, `"secret"`, `"omg"`, `"bro"`, `"look"`, `"banned"`, `"police"`, `"heist"`, `"hack"`, `"money"`.
* **Zero Low-Value Content:**
  - Reject silent walking, waiting in menus, inventory management, or reading comments without high-energy reactions.
