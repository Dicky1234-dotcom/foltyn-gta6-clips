# iart-ai Motion Graphics Skills Pack

A comprehensive suite of specialized motion design skills developed by [iart.ai](https://iart.ai) for AI coding agents. It encodes professional motion design craft, timing, safe areas, easing curves, and visual verification into agent workflows.

## Installed Specialized Skills

The pack includes the following individual skills installed into your Antigravity environment:

1. **`animation-principles`**
   - Timing, easing curves, mass/weight, squash and stretch, overshoot, secondary motion, stagger offsets.
   - Converts emotional intent and visual narrative into concrete physics, durations, and cubic-bezier parameters.

2. **`beat-sync-editing`**
   - Precise rhythmic cutting, transients detection, keyframing to musical beats, and BPM grid synchronization.

3. **`color-motion`**
   - Palette transitions, chromatic contrast in animation, focal pacing, and brand color motion rules.

4. **`logo-animation`**
   - Signature brand reveals, SVG path morphing, emblem builds, and kinetic stings.

5. **`motion-art-direction`**
   - Visual hierarchy, stylistic consistency, pacing, motion guidelines, and moodboard translation.

6. **`motion-background`**
   - Ambient kinetic textures, looping canvas/CSS backgrounds, particles, and Remotion backdrop layers.

7. **`remotion-video`**
   - Deterministic programmatic video in React, `useCurrentFrame()`, interpolation, spring mechanics, and render orchestration.

8. **`shot-composition`**
   - Cinematic camera framing, safe zones, thirds grid, focal planes, and multi-aspect staging (16:9, 9:16, 1:1).

9. **`after-effects`**
   - Expressions, precomps, template structures, and Bodymovin / Lottie / JSON animation pipelines.

## Deliver-and-Verify Tooling

The pack includes verification scripts located in `./scripts/`:
- `contact-sheet.sh`: Generates multi-frame contact sheets for rapid visual inspection.
- `probe-mp4.sh`: Checks duration, bitrate, frame rate, audio channels, and keyframe intervals via ffprobe.
- `seek-shot.sh`: Extracts key transition frames to inspect alignment and artifacting.
